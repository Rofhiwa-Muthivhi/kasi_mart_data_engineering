## Business Context

Kasi Mart is a retail business that sells different types of products to customers across different provinces. The products are grouped into categories such as Electronics, Home, Fashion, and Beauty.

The Electronics category includes products such as Bluetooth speakers and power banks. The Home category includes products such as coffee mugs and desk lamps. The Fashion category includes products such as sneakers and denim jackets, while the Beauty category includes products such as moisturizers and face masks.

For this project, I am working with three datasets: Customers, Products, and Orders. The Customers table contains information about the customers, the Products table contains information about the products Kasi Mart sells, and the Orders table records the transactions made by customers.

The main purpose of this project is to use Snowflake as a cloud-based data platform to ingest the three datasets, understand the relationships between them using joins, and analyse the data to generate business insights. Through this project, I will gain practical experience in moving raw data into a cloud platform, connecting related tables, and using the data to understand customers, products, and sales performance.
